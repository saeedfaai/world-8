## W8-P01 — persistent, auditable multi-agent societies

**Title:** A Governed Shared-Kernel Architecture for Persistent, Auditable Multi-Agent Societies Beyond the LLM Session

**Research focus:** governance, persistence, auditability, accountable continuity, and shared-kernel architecture for long-lived multi-agent societies whose identity and control state must survive beyond any one LLM session or provider.

**Current track:** submitted to *Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems* (Springer Nature). The submission is currently under consideration. Springer Nature In Review is enabled and the corresponding Research Square preprint is in production. The public Research Square URL/DOI will be added here only after it is issued.

## W8-P02 — forecast, decision, and order separation

**Title:** Forecast, Decision, and Order as Separate Objects: A Contract-Based Evaluation Architecture for Multi-Agent Market Systems

**Research focus:** explicit separation of Forecast, Decision, and Order; immutable forecast contracts; probabilistic calibration; receipt-backed evaluation; downstream decision boundaries; and evidence-bounded empirical claims.

**Empirical claim boundary:** the frozen replay supports explicit probabilistic calibration and receipt-backed forecast evaluation. It does **not** establish trading profitability, production readiness, universal cross-market superiority, causal superiority of the architecture, or autonomous market intelligence.

**Active tracks:**

1. **SSRN preprint/distribution track** — Abstract ID **7359740**, currently under staff review:

2. **Journal of Forecasting (Wiley)** — submitted to the editorial office on **2026-08-28**. Journal manuscript ID will be added once Wiley issues it.

## Publication policy

- **Journal submission is not acceptance.** Status wording on this page is intentionally conservative.
- **Preprint and journal tracks are distinguished.** A preprint URL or DOI does not imply peer-review acceptance.
- **No duplicate-paper inflation.** Multiple distribution/review tracks for the same manuscript are counted once at the manuscript level.
- **Append-only lineage.** Older public records and DOIs are preserved rather than overwritten.
- **Evidence-bounded claims.** Public communication must remain within the evidence ceiling declared by each manuscript/release.
