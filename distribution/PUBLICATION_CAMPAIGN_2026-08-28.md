## Medium / blog — short introduction

### World 8 Research Program: From Persistent Agent Societies to Evidence-Bounded Forecasting

World 8 is now being explored through two distinct research manuscripts.

The first asks a foundational systems question: can a multi-agent society remain persistent, governed, and auditable even when the LLM session or cognitive provider changes? This manuscript is currently under consideration at *Autonomous Agents and Multi-Agent Systems* (Springer Nature), with its In Review preprint in production at Research Square.

The second asks a forecasting question: what happens when Forecast, Decision, and Order are treated as separate evidence-bearing objects rather than collapsed into one agent output? That manuscript is moving through both an SSRN preprint/distribution track and a journal submission to *Journal of Forecasting* (Wiley).

The two papers share a common principle: **separate claims from evidence, and separate durable system identity or decisions from replaceable model output.**
